# Ownership and Reuse

This repository is an **OSYSTIC** public engineering case study.

## Ownership

The case-study text, diagrams, organization, and presentation are maintained by OSYSTIC.

The confidential Pine Script implementation is not included here and remains outside this public repository.

## Permitted public use

You may:

- view the repository;
- link to it;
- cite it with attribution;
- reference it as evidence of OSYSTIC engineering capability.

## Not permitted without written permission

You may not:

- represent the work as your own;
- copy the case study into another company's portfolio;
- white-label or resell the documentation;
- imply access to or rights over the confidential Pine source;
- use OSYSTIC branding to imply endorsement.
